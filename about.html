About Asian Business Cabletow Cooperative Academy

(ABCCA)

What is the Asian Business Cooperative Academy (ABCCA)?

Asian Business Cabletow Cooperative Academy (ABCCA) is a non-profit educational institution committed to providing accessible vocational training to individuals who aspire to build a career in welding. With a strong focus on practical skills and industry-relevant knowledge, ABCCA offers free training programs in Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW) NC1 and NC2. These programs are carefully designed to teach students both the foundational and advanced techniques of welding, preparing them for real-world applications in various industries.

ABCCA’s mission is to empower students by combining hands-on experience with safety awareness and professional standards, ensuring that every graduate is confident and competent in their craft. By making high-quality technical education available at no cost, the academy opens doors for personal growth and employment opportunities, fostering a community of skilled, responsible, and motivated professionals ready to contribute to the workforce and society.

Career Opportunities

After completing SMAW NC1 and NC2, graduates can explore a variety of career paths in the welding industry.

🧰

Welder / Fabricator

Working in construction, shipbuilding, pipelines, manufacturing, or metal fabrication shops.

🔧

Maintenance Technician

Performing welding repairs and maintenance on machinery, equipment, and structures.

🛠️

Pipe Welder

Specializing in welding pipes for industries such as oil, gas, and plumbing.

🏗️

Structural Welder

Fabricating and assembling structural steel for buildings, bridges, and other infrastructure projects.

🔍

Welding Inspector / Quality Control

Assisting in inspecting welds for compliance with safety and quality standards.

🏢

Entrepreneur / Small Business Owner

Starting a small welding or fabrication business, offering services to local clients.
